The equation
Itch = β₁S + β₂Aδ + β₃C − β₄Aβ + Ω

Itch is not a single signal. It is the net output of four competing fibre populations plus a nonlinear coupling term that determines how they amplify each other. Most people run this equation and get a small answer. You run it and get a large one — not because the inputs are different, but because your Ω is larger.

Aβ
Suppressor
Thick myelinated. Fast. Touch, pressure, vibration. Enters the equation with a minus sign. Your covering-and-waiting protocol activates these. The blanket is medicine. The thermo shirt is medicine. This is gate control — Aβ floods the dorsal horn and closes the gate on C-fibre traffic.
Aδ
Thermal sentinel
Thin myelinated. Faster than C. Detects transitions, not steady states. When you leave the shower (hot → cool air) or return to bed (warm skin → cool sheets), Aδ fires: something changed. Not itch yet — just alert.
C
Core itch carrier
Unmyelinated. Slow (0.3–1 m/s). Diffuse. Hard to localise. This is the burning, prickling, lingering quality you feel. In aquagenic pruritus these are firing near-spontaneously in response to water + evaporation + thermogradient. Non-histaminergic — antihistamines fail for a reason.
Ω
Coupling term
Not a fibre. A higher-order interaction among S, Aδ, C, humidity, sleep state, attention, and circadian phase. Two people with identical first-order values diverge because their Ω differs. This is the ghost that medicine forgets to name.

What Ω actually is

The coupling term that medicine forgets

The full Ukubona equation is:

log(x)nonself = [β₀ + β₁S]input + [β₂Vᵢ]throughput

Ω is not noise. Ω is not measurement error. Ω is the structured interaction among every compartment — the term that captures how a boda-boda ride plus a cold wind plus an argument produces a lethal coronary spasm in someone whose individual risk factors were unremarkable.

The back problem

The back is the one large skin surface evolution left socially dependent. Fewer sebaceous glands than face/scalp. Hard to inspect. Hard to moisturise. Easy to overheat in bed. The back is not where itch starts — it is where the deficit accumulates because the repair mechanism is structurally absent for solo-living humans.

Dermatology has independently rediscovered marriage as a moisturiser delivery system. The engineering solution is a long-handled silicone applicator. At 46 years unmoistured, this is no longer optional — it is the one parameter in the equation you have not yet optimised.
Why Uganda specifically adds a new Ω term

In Baltimore winter: dry air, cold air, heavy clothing, large thermal gradients but in a predictable direction. Your Ω was calibrated for that landscape. Uganda adds: higher ambient humidity, tropical night temperatures, lighter bedding, different water chemistry, and the specific interaction of humidity × sweat × sleep that creates a new coupling term your parameter set had never encountered. The bed-return itch appearing here is not a worsening — it is a new interaction being discovered.
